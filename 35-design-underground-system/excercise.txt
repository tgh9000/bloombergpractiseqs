PR: Add UndergroundSystem for journey time analytics
Summary
This change adds an UndergroundSystem class that records passenger check-ins and check-outs and reports average travel times between stations.

Expected behavior
check_in(id, station_name, t): passenger id checks in at station_name at time t. A passenger can only be checked in to one place at a time.
check_out(id, station_name, t): passenger id, who is currently checked in, checks out at station_name at time t. This completes one journey from the check-in station to station_name taking (t - check-in time).
After checking out, the passenger is no longer checked in and may later check in again (the same id can make many journeys).
get_average_time(start_station, end_station) returns the average travel time, as a float, over every completed journey that went directly from start_station to end_station.
Direction matters: journeys from A to B are tracked separately from journeys from B to A. get_average_time(A, B) only includes journeys that started at A and ended at B.
The average must be exact (for example, journeys of 5, 6 and 9 minutes average 6.666..., not 6).
get_average_time is only called for station pairs with at least one completed journey.
Station names are 1 to 10 English letters and digits. Times are integers from 0 to 10^6, and check-out time is always after check-in time.
Every method should run in O(1) average time.
